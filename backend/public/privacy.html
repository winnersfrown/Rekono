1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how [Your legal business name] ("Rekono," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Rekono invoice extraction and reconciliation service (the "Service"), including this dashboard application and the marketing site you arrived from.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and organization name. If you sign up or sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and whether Google has verified that email, but nothing else from your Google account. If you sign up with a password instead, we store a cryptographically hashed version of it (via bcrypt) — we never store or have access to your actual password.

Onboarding answers. The short questionnaire after signup (your role, company size, primary use case, and typical monthly invoice volume) is stored against your organization to help us understand how the Service is used.

Uploaded documents and extracted data. The invoices, purchase orders, and bank statements you upload are stored, along with the vendor names, amounts, dates, line items, and other structured data we extract from them, any corrections you make during review, and the resulting match results against your POs/bank records. This is the core data the Service exists to process.

Billing information. If you choose a paid plan, payment is handled entirely by our payment processor, Stripe — we never see or store your card number. We do store a reference to your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your chosen plan, and your subscription status, so we know what you're entitled to.

Usage and log data. Like most web services, our hosting infrastructure logs standard request data (IP address, timestamps, endpoints called) for security and debugging purposes.

Cookies and local storage. We use one short-lived, essential cookie during Google sign-in to prevent cross-site request forgery (cleared immediately after); it isn't used for tracking or advertising. Your session token is stored in your browser's local storage, not a cookie, and is used to keep you signed in. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

3. How we use this information

  • To provide the Service: extracting, storing, and letting you review and reconcile your invoices.
  • To operate your account: authentication, plan enforcement, billing, and customer support.
  • To improve the extraction and matching logic (in aggregate; we don't use your specific documents to train any external model — see Section 4 on our use of Google's Gemini API).
  • To send you service-related email: password resets, billing receipts and failures, and, if you contact us, replies to that conversation. We don't send marketing email unless you separately opt in somewhere that says so.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the service providers that make Rekono work, each acting on our behalf under their own privacy/security commitments:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google — only if you use "Sign in with Google," to authenticate you.
  • Google (Gemini API) — if configured on this deployment, the text of uploaded documents is sent to Google's Gemini API to extract structured data. Data sent to the Gemini API is not used to train Google's models by default. If this deployment doesn't have a Gemini API key configured, extraction falls back to a local, non-AI heuristic and nothing is sent to Google's Gemini API at all.
  • Resend — sends transactional email on our behalf (password resets, contact form replies).
  • Our hosting/infrastructure provider(s) — hosts the application and database.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Rekono, our users, or others.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and document data for as long as your account is active, so your audit trail and historical records stay intact. If you'd like your account and its data deleted, email us at wfrownusa@yahoo.com and we'll process the request — there is no self-service deletion button in the product today. We may retain limited records where required for legal, tax, or audit obligations even after a deletion request.

6. Data security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Access tokens are signed and expire after 14 days. Every piece of data is scoped to your organization, so other customers' accounts cannot see it. Traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) in production. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your account details from within the Service, or contact us at wfrownusa@yahoo.com for anything else, including a full data export or deletion request.

8. Children's privacy

Rekono is a business tool and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

How it works

One pipeline, from inbox to reconciled record.

Five steps, in order — each one a real stage the document passes through, not a marketing simplification.

01

Upload

Drop in a PDF or a photo of an invoice, one at a time or as a batch. It's queued immediately and processed in the background — nothing blocks on the upload.

async job queue
02

Read

OCR lifts the raw text from the page, then a language model parses it into a fixed schema — vendor, dates, PO reference, line items, tax, and totals.

OCR + structured extraction
03

Check

Line items are summed and compared against the stated total. Every field also carries its own self-reported confidence score.

automatic cross-check
04

Review

Anything below the confidence bar — or that fails the cross-check — lands in the review queue. Source document and extracted fields sit side by side; corrections are one click and get logged.

human-in-the-loop
05

Reconcile & export

Approved invoices are fuzzy-matched against your PO list or bank statement — vendor name, amount tolerance, date window — then exported to CSV or Excel, or synced onward.

matching engine + export

FAQ

Questions worth answering before you upload anything.

What file types does Rekono accept?+
PDF and common image formats — PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP, WEBP. If you can scan it or photograph it, Rekono can read it.
What happens to invoices Rekono isn't confident about?+
They're routed to the review queue instead of auto-approved. You see the source document and the extracted fields side by side, correct whatever's off, and the correction is written to the audit log.
How does matching actually work?+
Rekono fuzzy-matches vendor name, amount (within a tolerance you set — percentage or flat dollar), and a date window against an uploaded PO list or bank statement, plus an exact PO/reference check when one is present on the invoice.
Can I try it without an API key?+
Yes. Without a key, Rekono falls back to a heuristic extractor so you can run the full pipeline end to end. Production-grade extraction runs on a Claude API key you provide.
Is there an audit trail?+
Every extraction, human correction, approval, rejection, and match decision is written to an audit log with a timestamp and who did it — the record accounting and compliance conversations ask for.
Do you integrate with QuickBooks, Xero, or NetSuite?+
Not yet. CSV and Excel export ship today, built on the same data those integrations will need — direct sync is next on the roadmap as we work with early customers.
Can I self-host it?+
Yes — Rekono runs on Postgres or SQLite and ships with a Dockerfile and docker-compose setup for running it entirely on your own infrastructure.
What happens if I go over my document cap?+
Uploads pause once you hit your plan's cap for the month, and you're prompted to upgrade right there if you want to keep going -- no surprise per-document charges. Your cap resets automatically at the start of the next month either way.
